About Kyoto

Kyoto was Japan's capital for over a thousand years. It is famous for its classical Buddhist temples, gardens, imperial palaces, Shinto shrines, and traditional wooden houses.

"Kyoto is the cultural heart of Japan, a place where history and tradition live in perfect harmony with nature."

Must-Visit Attractions

1. Fushimi Inari Shrine

Famous for its thousands of vermilion torii gates, which straddle a network of trails behind its main buildings.

2. Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)

A Zen temple whose top two floors are completely covered in gold leaf, overlooking a serene pond.

3. Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Walking through the soaring bamboo stalks feels like stepping into another world altogether.

Quick Travel Tips

  • Get an IC Card (ICOCA or Suica) for smooth public transit travel.
  • Visit popular temples early in the morning to avoid large crowds.
  • Respect local customs, especially in traditional areas like Gion.
